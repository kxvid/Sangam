# Sangam operations guide

Use **Operations** at `/operations` after signing in. This private pilot automates research processing, explanations and work preparation. You still negotiate, approve, mail, dial and close. Estimated profit is not collected revenue.

## First practice test

1. Select **Sangam Practice — fictional data only**. Keep invented names, addresses and documents clearly labeled. The separate **Practice** view in Operations shows fictional workflows; readiness always reports real business results.
2. Use the existing Acquisition import workflow or the paused fictional source in **Sourcing & matching**. To repeat the latter exercise, review and reactivate that fictional source, upload the template with invented records, publish the original seller and buyer rules, and inspect Matches. Real source permissions must be evidenced separately.
3. In **Profit scenarios**, select a fictional property. Enter a base case, downside and upside. Blanks mean unknown. Example assumptions: ARV $400,000, repairs $40,000, contingency 10%, buyer closing/holding costs $15,000, buyer profit margin 20%, seller price $240,000, buyer price $260,000, target assignment fee $15,000, operator closing costs $1,000, marketing $2,000 and other deal costs $500. The modeled buyer ceiling is $261,000; maximum seller offer $246,000; projected operator net $16,500 before tax/unallocated overhead. Saving does not send an offer or replace comp-based underwriting.
4. Open **Daily work**. Tasks come from actual saved records, missing evidence and deadlines. A task disappears only when the underlying record resolves it. Links take you to the Acquisition desk; they never dial, send or approve anything.
5. For buyer tracking, use a fictional signed-contract workflow and a fictional buyer packet in Acquisition. In **Buyer follow-up**, select that relationship and plan a callback. Then record a fictional conversation or received offer with a clearly fictional reference. A plan does not count as a delivery. Received offers can be explicitly withdrawn; they do not become assignments or revenue.
6. Reload. Scenarios and buyer history should persist. Return to **Real** view and **Readiness**: practice records must not launch the 90-day clock, add offers/contracts to real targets or increase collected fees.

## Daily operating rhythm

- Review failed/held sourcing jobs, source expiration and changed decisions in Sourcing & matching.
- In Daily work, resolve elapsed/approaching closings first, then buyer alerts and follow-ups, seller qualification, underwriting and reviewed outreach preparation.
- Compare economics before negotiating. Test higher repair costs and lower resale value. Check the assumption notes and three sold comparable references in Acquisition.
- Use the existing reviewed mail export or manual calling sheet. Record the actual outcome afterward, including opt-outs. Never record a planned action as completed.
- Record buyer replies and next follow-up dates; compare received offers with your scenario. Execute assignments and closings outside the app, then record their genuine document/payment references.
- Reconcile real costs and collected assignment fees. Readiness shows current inventory, all-time recorded cash and the launched 90-day pilot separately.

## Free data

**Census address lookup** is available per property after an explicit checkbox/click. Only the property street, city, state and ZIP go to Census. It returns approximate address suggestions without changing the stored property, motivation score or contact permission. The workspace limit is 20 attempts in a rolling 24 hours and one attempt per property per minute, including failed requests. Results remain on the screen until you leave or refresh; the request is audited. A failed lookup does not erase facts.

**FEMA county context** is implemented as a held adapter until current usage terms can be verified. **Riverside permits** remains held because the documented feed returned an access error and its machine fields could not be verified. Neither supplies proven distressed sellers. See [Free data sources](FREE-DATA-SOURCES.md) for dated primary references and limits.

PropStream still requires your authorized export. Scheduled refresh re-evaluates uploaded records; it does not fetch a new PropStream list.

## Before inviting paying customers

The current release is a private pilot with administrator-provisioned workspaces. Customer billing, self-service onboarding and production backup/restore evidence are separate release requirements. No paid subscription was started for this build. Verify operating expenses, support ownership and source rights for each customer before selling a managed or self-service service. The app does not promise profit or remove negotiation and closing work.
